About the science and this game

Inspired by On the estimation and validity of AI time horizons by Drew T. Nguyen and William Fithian. Their paper argues that converting human task time into AI task difficulty is not simply linear: its fitted spline is nearly flat from 2–30 minutes and closer to linear elsewhere. This game turns that insight into a comparison puzzle.

The curve here is an illustrative hand-shaped teaching curve, not a fitted estimate or the paper's METR results. It uses difficulty points with arbitrary units, no benchmark data, no model fitting, and no claim of predictive accuracy. The 75% forecast is fixed for every chosen answer so log and Brier scores can show how proper scoring rules reward calibrated forecasts. Independent educational implementation; not endorsed by the authors. No third-party code, data, weights, or assets are included.
